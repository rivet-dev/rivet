## Where a Rivet Actor stays

A Rivet Actor does not move between regions. Reads and writes to its data never cross a region boundary. A call between Rivet Actors in different regions goes over the network, so treat it like any other remote call.

## Self-hosting

A self-hosted control plane runs in one region. Multi-region self-hosting is coming.
