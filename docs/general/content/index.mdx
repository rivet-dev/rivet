Rivet runs agents, workflows, sandboxes, and generated apps as **Rivet Actors**: long-lived processes with durable state. One control plane schedules them, routes requests to them, and persists their state. Your code runs in workers you operate, on your own infrastructure or on [Rivet Cloud](https://dashboard.rivet.dev).
