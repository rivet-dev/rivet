The control plane checks credentials before serving management requests or routing Actor traffic. Application-level authorization still belongs in your Actor. See [Authentication](/actors/docs/authentication).

## Self-hosted control plane

The configured `auth.admin_token` grants every operation in every namespace. Keep it in your backend or secret store. Use it to create namespaces and issue [scoped JWTs](/docs/jwt) for applications.

A JWT grants access to one namespace and an explicit set of resources and operations. Give browser clients only the permissions they need, and keep tokens that can register nodes or manage pools on your backend.

Pass credentials in `Authorization: Bearer <token>`, or use an [endpoint URL](/docs/endpoints) such as `https://production:<token>@rivet.mycompany.com`.

## Rivet Cloud

Get your namespace endpoints and credentials from the [Rivet Cloud dashboard](https://dashboard.rivet.dev). Keep secret credentials on your backend. See [Rivet Cloud](/docs/deploy/cloud/) for managed deployment and token settings.

## Inspector

The Actor inspector has a separate credential. See [Debugging Actors](/actors/docs/debugging#inspector-endpoints).
